The Bot is an anti-scam/anti-spam "honeypot" tool. It posts a decoy channel intended to attract malicious accounts, challenges accounts that interact with it using a captcha, and — depending on the outcome and the configuration chosen by the Server's admins — may warn, mute, softban, or ban the account and notify the Server's moderators.
